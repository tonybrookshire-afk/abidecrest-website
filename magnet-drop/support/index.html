Player support

Magnet Drop: Polarity

Gameplay guidance, troubleshooting, and Premium purchase help.

How to play

Navigate the ball through alternating red and green gates. In touch mode, hold to steer right and release to steer left. Tilt controls are available in settings.

Choose Easy, Normal, or Expert. Aim for perfect passes to earn bonus points and build your streak.

Premium and Restore Purchases

If you previously purchased Premium using your Apple Account, open the game's purchase screen and select Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

If restoration doesn't work, verify your App Store account and internet connection, then restart the game and try again. Purchases are handled by Apple.

Rewarded ads and revives

Optional rewarded ads depend on network access and ad availability. If you see No Ad Available, you can retry later or end the run. Ads are not guaranteed to be available for every attempt.

Need additional help?

For technical problems, include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, game build number, and a brief description of what happened.
