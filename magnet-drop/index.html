Good to know

Quick answers

Is Magnet Drop free?

Yes. Magnet Drop: Polarity is free to download. The game includes optional advertising and a Premium in-app purchase.

Can I play on iPhone and iPad?

Yes. Magnet Drop: Polarity is available for supported iPhone and iPad devices running iOS or iPadOS 15 or later.

Do I need an account?

No account is needed to play. Gameplay statistics are stored locally on your device; they do not currently sync between devices.

How do revives and Premium work?

Optional rewarded ads can provide revives when available. Premium provides two ad-free Second Chances per UTC day. The game allows up to five revives per run in total, including ad-free revives. Learn more on the support page.
