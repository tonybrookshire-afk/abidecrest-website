Polarity Quest

Flip polarity. Charge your shield.

Steer a magnetic ball through an endless field of disruptors. Time each switch, scoop up blue crystals, and see how long you can survive.

How to play ↓

How to play

Switch polarity

Red and green attract. Matching colors repel. Play with hold-and-release or tap-to-toggle controls.

Collect blue crystals

Each crystal earns 50 points and adds one shield charge when you have room.

Deflect disruptors

Your shield spends a charge to knock an incoming disruptor aside. Survive longer to beat your high score.

Choose Easy, Normal, or Expert. On Easy, you get three crystals before the first disruptor arrives.
