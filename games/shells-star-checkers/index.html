How to play

Red starts. Tap your marble, then an empty neighboring hole, or jump over another marble into the empty hole beyond it. You can chain jumps in one turn by choosing any reachable landing hole. Jumped marbles stay on the board.

First to fill the opposite triangle with all ten marbles wins. No timer, no rush!
