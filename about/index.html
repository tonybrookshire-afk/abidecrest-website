Who we are

AbideCrest Media is an independent creative studio bringing imagination, thoughtful design, and technology together to create meaningful experiences.

Our story

It starts with an idea.

AbideCrest Media began with a simple goal: build something enjoyable, create it with care, and keep making it better. That idea grew into an independent studio focused on games and digital experiences that are easy to discover, rewarding to learn, and worth returning to.

We believe small beginnings can lead to meaningful things. Every project is an opportunity to learn, improve, and make something we're proud to share.

What we create

Games made for the joy of one more try.

Magnet Drop: Polarity

Our first mobile game challenges players to guide a ball through electrified gates, build streaks, and chase a new personal best. Choose Touch or Tilt controls and find your rhythm across three difficulty levels.

Version 1.1 has been submitted for App Store review. The update brings a refreshed welcome screen, improved controls, and gameplay refinements. Availability depends on Apple's review and release process.
